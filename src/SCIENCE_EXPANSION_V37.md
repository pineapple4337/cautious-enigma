# Version 37 — Science pathway expansion

Expanded five Science second-major and minor pathways beyond their original prefix-only placeholders, using departmental publications. Chemistry and Physics have the clearest published course baskets. Statistics has published detailed alternatives; Mathematics has a detailed second-major PDF. Life Sciences pathways remain provisional pending complete cohort-specific basket extraction.

All ten remain marked `partial` until group allocations, substitution chains and exclusions are fully represented. In particular, an eligible-course total can overcount a course from the wrong basket. This release does not claim that all 15 Science pathways are certified.
