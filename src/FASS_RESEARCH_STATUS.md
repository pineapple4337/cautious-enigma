## Before declaring a pathway Supported

1. Verify official requirements against the correct matriculation cohort.
2. Encode every compulsory, OR, level, recognised-course and cap rule.
3. Confirm non-standard requirements (field placements, grades, language proficiency, thesis/approval).
4. Add valid and invalid synthetic-plan tests and run the full build.
5. Do not silently mark a pathway Supported when its audit is incomplete.
