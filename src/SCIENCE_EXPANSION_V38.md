# Version 38 — Four specialised Science primary majors

Added cohort AY2025/26 definitions for Quantitative Finance, Food Science and Technology, Pharmaceutical Science, and Data Science and Analytics. All four are deliberately marked **partial**, not fully supported. The source rules are encoded as explicit compulsory courses, alternative baskets and advanced units.

## Important engine limitations

- DSA: Level-4000 completion is an **either/or** rule (two advanced courses from separate baskets **or** an 8-unit Honours project). The generic engine currently shows both branches, so its two advanced-course checks should **not** be interpreted as both mandatory.
- FST/PHS/DSA: Approved NOC/UPIP internships can satisfy experiential requirements; a course-code-only audit cannot infer all those alternatives.
- FST: Specialisations in Research and Innovation / Industrial Applications remain separate future definitions.
- QF: QF4288 is an 8-unit project; do not infer 4 units for it.
- The AY2025/26 programme name is Data Science and Analytics; Data Science and Applied AI is for AY2026/27 onwards.
