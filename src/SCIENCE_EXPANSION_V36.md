# Version 36 — Faculty of Science research-first expansion

Added five AY2025/26 primary-major draft audits (Chemistry, Life Sciences, Mathematics, Physics, Statistics), plus provisional second-major/minor entries for the same subjects. All 15 are explicitly marked **partial / unsupported** in the registry. The remaining pathways (DSA, QF, FST, PHS, DSE, Environmental Studies, and specialised minors) are researched but not falsely represented as complete.

Important: the current generic engine cannot represent all multi-basket allocations, UROPS caps, internships, specialisations and equivalences. Some numeric checks are lower-bound approximations; a green check on a partial audit is NOT certification.
