# Version 40: Cross-disciplinary programmes and Science specialisations

## Data Science and Economics (AY2021–2025)
Official PDF: https://www.stat.nus.edu.sg/wp-content/uploads/sites/8/2024/06/120624-Major-Data-Science-and-Economics-Programme-Requirements-AY21-22-and-after.pdf

96-unit major, 44 CHS/GE units, 20 unrestricted electives. Includes the prescribed compulsory sequence, two alternative prerequisite baskets and four advanced electives. DSE4201 excluded under the no-approval assumption. Advanced elective equivalence and overlap checks are not yet exhaustive.

## Environmental Studies (AY2024 onwards)
Official PDF: https://envstudies.nus.edu.sg/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/04/FoS_BES-XDP-Cohort-AY24-onwards-revised.pdf

88 programme units, 40 CHS, 32 unrestricted electives. Tracks integrated-core options, field course, Life Sciences/Geography level distribution, capstone alternative and ENV-recognised basket. Does not yet enforce exclusive allocation of every course across baskets.

## Science specialisations
Verified Astrophysics and Pure Mathematics course baskets are available as provisional selections. Other FoS specialisations are not yet implemented. Specialisation overlap limits need dedicated allocation logic.

## Important
DSE and BES use nonstandard CHS allocations. The generic 52-unit CHS audit is intentionally not applied to them. Neither is labelled fully supported until the CHS pathway and course-allocation checks are complete.
